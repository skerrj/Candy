## Where things stand

How the owner reviews work today (`doc/process.md`, "The branch lifecycle", steps 3 and 5):

- each step adds `- [ ] Review step N as built` to `TODOS.md`, pointing at its HLD's
  *Done. As built:* note; the owner works through them in the chat, a step at a time, and
  they are ticked (sometimes with a verdict: "looks ok for now");
- leaks, tech debt and HLD questions are reviewed the same way, by reading the file;
- `TODOS.md` is archived when the next branch starts, its open items carried over (some
  for several branches: "Carried over (for owner review)").
